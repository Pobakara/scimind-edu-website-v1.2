Privacy, clearly explained.

How SciMind Education handles personal information when you visit our website or contact us about science programmes.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

At SciMind Education, we respect your privacy and recognise the importance of protecting personal information about parents, guardians, students and visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect information when you visit our website or communicate with us.

1. About SciMind Education

SciMind Education is operated by Nisansala Bandarayapa, Yapa Mudiyanselage, as a sole trader (ABN: 73 363 895 731).

We provide specialist science education through online classes and face-to-face learning at 23 Highfield Avenue, Mulgrave VIC 3170, Australia. You can contact us at info@scimind.com.au.

This policy covers our public website at scimind.com.au and initial enquiries. Separate enrolment, student administration and learning platforms may have additional privacy arrangements.

2. Personal information we collect

When you contact SciMind Education, we may collect:

  • Your name (including a parent or guardian's name)
  • Email address and telephone number, if provided
  • Student year level and subject or programme of interest
  • Preferred learning format, if provided
  • Information you choose to include in a message
  • Related correspondence about your enquiry

Our website and security services may also process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser and device information, request timestamps and security verification results.

Please avoid including sensitive information, medical details or identification documents in a general website enquiry.

3. How information is collected

We collect information when you submit a website contact or quick enquiry form, email us, contact us through another communication channel, or voluntarily provide details about our educational services. Technical information may be processed automatically when you use the website or submit an enquiry.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with parents and guardians
  • Provide details about classes, programmes and enrolment arrangements
  • Maintain relevant correspondence and administrative records
  • Protect our website against spam, misuse and unauthorised activity
  • Meet applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements

We do not sell or rent personal information. Submitting an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to promotional mailing lists.

5. Service providers

We use selected technology providers to operate the public website and handle enquiries:

  • Cloudflare: supports website and enquiry security, bot checks through Turnstile, server-side processing, rate limiting and related technical operations.
  • Resend: delivers website enquiry notifications to SciMind Education's business email address.
  • Google Workspace: receives, stores and manages business email correspondence.

When you consent to analytics, Google also processes website usage information through Google Analytics 4 as described in section 10.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services, under their applicable service terms and privacy practices.

6. Sharing and overseas processing

Website enquiries are managed by SciMind Education. We do not routinely share personal information submitted through our enquiry forms with Vinsara Institute of Education.

We may share information with authorised service providers to operate our website or manage communications, or disclose it where required or authorised by law.

Although SciMind Education operates in Australia, some service providers use infrastructure outside Australia. For example, Resend processes or stores email-related information in the United States. Other providers may also process information internationally according to their service arrangements.

We do not provide personal information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Children's and students' privacy

Our public enquiry forms are primarily intended for parents and guardians seeking information about programmes for school-aged students. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise information shared about children and avoid providing unnecessary sensitive details.

Additional information needed for enrolment or educational services will be handled under the relevant administrative arrangements.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss and inappropriate disclosure. Measures used in our enquiry system include server-side validation, automated spam protection and restricted access to service credentials.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed to respond to enquiries, maintain relevant business communications, carry out legitimate administration and meet applicable legal obligations.

SciMind Education periodically reviews enquiry records and correspondence. Where information is no longer reasonably required, we take appropriate steps to securely delete or otherwise manage it, subject to legal requirements and service-provider backup arrangements.

10. Cookies, analytics and website technologies

Our website may use essential technical mechanisms for operation and security. Cloudflare Turnstile and related security services may process browser, device and network information to help detect automated submissions.

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google, to understand how visitors use our website. GA4 may collect page views, interactions, browser and device information, language, approximate location and technical identifiers. Google may use cookies, including _ga and _ga_*, to distinguish visitors and sessions. Google states that GA4 uses IP addresses transiently to derive approximate location and does not log or store them. Google may process this information outside Australia under its applicable privacy and service terms.

Analytics is optional and remains off until you select Accept analytics. If you reject, GA4 is not loaded and analytics cookies are not used. If you withdraw consent after accepting, the tag receives an updated denied-consent state, analytics cookies are removed where accessible, and no further page-view or enquiry events are sent by this website; the already-loaded tag remains in the current page until you leave or reload. On later page loads, GA4 stays unloaded while consent is denied. Your choice is stored in this browser's local storage, not in an analytics cookie. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer. When analytics is permitted, we measure page views and successful enquiry submissions using page URLs without query parameters. Enquiry events include only the form category and page URL; names, contact details and message contents are not sent to Google Analytics.

We do not use Google Ads tracking or advertising cookies. Consent Mode v2 is configured to deny analytics and advertising storage by default; optional analytics is activated only after consent. Essential website and security services, including Cloudflare Turnstile, operate independently of this optional analytics choice.

11. External websites

Our website may link to independently operated services, including Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and educational collaboration partners. Their privacy policies apply when you visit or use their services.

12. Access and correction

You can contact us to ask for access to personal information we hold about you or to request correction of inaccurate or outdated information. We may need to verify your identity before providing or changing information, to protect the privacy of everyone involved.

Send requests to info@scimind.com.au.

13. Privacy questions and complaints

If you have a privacy question, concern or complaint, please contact:

SciMind Education — Privacy Enquiries
Email: info@scimind.com.au
Address: 23 Highfield Avenue, Mulgrave VIC 3170, Australia

Please provide sufficient details for us to understand and investigate your concern. We aim to respond to privacy complaints within 30 days.

Where applicable, you may also seek guidance or lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

14. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, practices or applicable legal requirements change. The current version will be published here with its most recent update date.
