## Before and after launch

**Before launch:** Check titles, descriptions, one H1 per page, navigation, canonical scheme/host, robots/sitemap contents, schema facts, responsive images, alt text, media loading, error pages, approved privacy policy and permissioned testimonials. Check only the pages intended for indexing are indexable.

**After launch:** Verify domain with Google Search Console, submit `/sitemap.xml`, inspect key URLs, configure Bing Webmaster Tools, run mobile PageSpeed Insights and assess Core Web Vitals. Add GA4 or advertising conversion tags only with a reviewed privacy/consent plan. Google Business Profile details must accurately describe the real business location/service arrangements.

## Reviews, results and minors

- Preserve original message wording and meaning. Do not fabricate quotes, reviewers, five-star ratings or outcomes.
- Anonymous attribution reduces identification risk but does **not automatically grant publication permission** for private student/parent messages.
- Keep original screenshots and identifiable student/school information out of Git and public site. Review contextual clues that could reidentify a student.
- For a parent/child message, request suitable consent before publicising a direct quotation or identifiable award. Keep a private approval record with exact approved excerpt and any agreed attribution.
- A Junior Science Olympiad result is an individual achievement, not a SciMind award or a promise that other students will earn the same result.
- `assets/content/testimonials.json`: `approved: true` must only represent actual documented publication permission; local design previews may be reset before commit.
